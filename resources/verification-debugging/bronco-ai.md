## English

[Bronco AI](https://www.bronco.ai/) — Commercial multi-agent platform for specification review, RTL and assertions, UVM testbench bring-up, regression triage, simulation debugging, and coverage closure. The vendor describes shared project context, findings linked to specifications, execution on customer Slurm/LSF compute, and on-premises deployment with configurable models.

Capabilities and customer performance figures are vendor-reported. The separately linked DVBench conference announcement does not establish that benchmark data or an evaluation harness are publicly released. The platform's first-public date is not established by the linked product page.

## 简体中文

[Bronco AI](https://www.bronco.ai/) — 商业多 Agent 平台，覆盖规格审查、RTL 与断言、UVM 测试平台搭建、回归分类、仿真调试及覆盖率闭合。厂商介绍了共享项目上下文、关联规格的调查结果、客户 Slurm/LSF 算力执行，以及可配置模型的本地部署。

功能与客户性能数据为厂商报告。另行关联的 DVBench 会议公告不代表基准数据或评测工具已公开发布；所链接产品页未确定平台首次公开日期。
