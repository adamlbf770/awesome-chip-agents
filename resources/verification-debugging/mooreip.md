## English

[MooreIP](https://www.mooreslab.ai/families/mooreip) — MooresLabAI's commercial suite for specification analysis, UVM verification, debugging, and coverage workflows. The [VerifAgent product page](https://www.mooreslab.ai/products/mooreip-verifagent) describes specification intake, test planning, testbench generation, simulation/debug iterations, and coverage closure with engineering review at each gate. It lists OpenAI and Anthropic integrations and existing commercial simulators.

The suite page marks DesignAgent and SignoffAgent as coming soon; the VerifAgent page lists on-premises LLM deployment as roadmap work. Capabilities and customer efficiency figures are vendor-reported. The linked pages do not establish a first-public date.

## 简体中文

[MooreIP](https://www.mooreslab.ai/families/mooreip) — MooresLabAI 面向规格分析、UVM 验证、调试与覆盖率流程的商业产品组。[VerifAgent 产品页](https://www.mooreslab.ai/products/mooreip-verifagent)介绍规格输入、测试计划、测试平台生成、仿真调试迭代及覆盖率闭合，并在各关口由工程师审查；页面列出 OpenAI、Anthropic 和现有商业仿真器集成。

产品组页面将 DesignAgent、SignoffAgent 标为即将推出；VerifAgent 页面将本地 LLM 部署列为路线图工作。功能与客户效率数据为厂商报告，所链接页面未确定首次公开日期。
