## English

[SigmanticAI](https://www.sigmanticai.com/) — Commercial chip-design agents for RTL and verification-IP generation, waveform analysis, debugging, and EDA-tool interaction. The official product page distinguishes Studio, an available interactive teammate, from Polaris, an autonomous engine marked Alpha; specification-to-silicon timing, physical-design, and signoff support remain later roadmap stages.

The [company's YC profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/sigmanticai) explicitly describes LLM-based HDL tooling and integration with existing synthesis tools. Capabilities and benchmark scores are vendor-reported. Company founding and YC batch dates are not used as product-release dates.

## 简体中文

[SigmanticAI](https://www.sigmanticai.com/) — 商业芯片设计 Agent，面向 RTL 与验证 IP 生成、波形分析、调试及 EDA 工具调用。官方产品页区分了已提供的交互式 Studio 与标记为 Alpha 的自主引擎 Polaris；规格到硅片所需的时序、物理设计和签核支持仍列为后续路线图阶段。

[公司 YC 页面](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/sigmanticai)明确介绍了基于 LLM 的 HDL 工具及现有综合工具集成。功能与基准分数为厂商报告；公司成立与 YC 批次日期不作为产品发布日期。
