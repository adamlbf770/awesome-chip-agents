## English

### Tools and Platforms

[Silimate](https://www.silimate.com/) — Commercial frontend design and debugging agents combining Fast Engines for ML-based PPA feedback with Chip Models built from fine-tuned LLMs. The [CTO overview](https://www.silimate.com/blog/podcast-interview-cto) distinguishes traditional ML for early PPA prediction from LLM reasoning over specifications, RTL, and testbenches.

The [2025 year-end review](https://www.silimate.com/blog/2025-year-end-review), published 2026-01-01, documents Preqorsor v1.0 for PPA prediction and optimization, and SMDB for AI debugging and root-cause analysis. Product capabilities and customer speedups are vendor-reported. The review date does not establish the platform's initial release date.

## 简体中文

### 工具与平台

[Silimate](https://www.silimate.com/) — 商业前端设计与调试 Agent，将提供 ML 驱动 PPA 反馈的 Fast Engines 与基于微调 LLM 的 Chip Models 结合。[CTO 概览](https://www.silimate.com/blog/podcast-interview-cto)区分了用于早期 PPA 预测的传统 ML，以及对规格、RTL 和测试平台进行推理的 LLM。

发表于 2026-01-01 的[2025 年回顾](https://www.silimate.com/blog/2025-year-end-review)记录了用于 PPA 预测与优化的 Preqorsor v1.0，以及用于 AI 调试和根因分析的 SMDB。产品能力与客户加速数据为厂商报告；回顾文章的日期不代表平台首次发布日期。
