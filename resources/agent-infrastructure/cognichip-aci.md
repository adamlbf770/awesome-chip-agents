## English

[Cognichip ACI Enterprise](https://www.cognichip.ai/product/aci) — Commercial chip-design system combining proprietary physics-informed models with an agent framework, persistent project context, tool/task orchestration, and inference infrastructure. Official workflow descriptions cover architecture exploration, RTL creation and modification, UVM verification and root-cause analysis, PPA optimization, and FPGA prototyping.

This record captures the published platform architecture and task scope. Model-quality, formal-correctness, coverage, and productivity claims are vendor-reported, without an independently reproduced evaluation linked here. The undated product pages do not establish the platform's first-public date.
